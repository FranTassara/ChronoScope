[3] Where tau lands on pure noise (white noise, 10 d, n=1000)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INTERIM (argmax Qp):  median tau 30.3 h, 52% of trials at tau >= 30 h
  NEW (argmin p):       median tau 25.0 h, 16% of trials at tau >= 30 h
  (A uniform spread over 18-32 h would put 14% at >= 30 h.)
  The null mean of Qp is its df = n_slots - 1, which grows with P, so
  the raw-Qp argmax drifts to the longest periods scanned whenever the
  rhythm is weak. Comparing periods on the p-value scale removes this.

Summary
  * OLD called 100% of pure-noise recordings rhythmic (threshold ~n_slots-
    fold too low).
  * INTERIM (the 2026-09-17 fix) still called ~95-97% of them rhythmic.
    The earlier version of this report described that rate as 'close to
    the nominal alpha'; that was wrong. Most of the excess came from
    comparing every period with one flat line at the median df; the rest
    from taking the best of many periods (look-elsewhere effect).
  * NEW holds the false-positive rate at the nominal 5% on white noise
    and on DAM-like count noise (see [1]). It is NOT fully calibrated on
    strongly autocorrelated Gaussian noise: ~10% at AR(1) phi = 0.5 and
    ~12% at phi = 0.8 (30-min bins), because dependence that outlasts the
    2-h blocks is partly broken by the permutation. This is still an
    8-10-fold reduction from INTERIM on the same series.
  * NEW reports tau without the long-period bias of the raw-Qp argmax
    (see [3]) and with smaller period error on rhythmic series (see [2]).
