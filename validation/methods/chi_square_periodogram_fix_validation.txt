[3] Residual caveat: multiple comparisons across the scanned period range
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The app reports significance by comparing the BEST Qp across ~280 scanned
  periods to a single per-period chi-square threshold. Even with the fixed
  calibration, this 'take the best of many correlated tests' procedure has a
  higher true false-positive rate than the nominal alpha (see [1], NEW column,
  still above 5% for short recordings). This look-elsewhere effect is a known
  property of periodogram peak-picking in general, is NOT the discontinuity
  bug reported by Tackenberg & Hughey, and is NOT fixed by this change. A full
  fix would require a permutation- or extreme-value-corrected threshold and is
  left as a documented limitation / future-work item.

Summary: replaced the Qp normalisation with the peer-reviewed 'greedy' CSP
formula (Tackenberg & Hughey 2021), which correctly recalibrates the
significance threshold (previously off by roughly n_slots-fold, causing
near-universal false 'significant rhythmicity' calls) and removes the
internally-derived approximation in favour of a validated reference formula.
True-positive period recovery on rhythmic signals is unchanged. A residual,
distinct multiple-testing caveat over the scanned period range remains and
is disclosed above, not fixed by this change.
