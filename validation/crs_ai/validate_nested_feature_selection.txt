  Run date: 2026-09-16 09:42
  This is a supplementary robustness analysis. It does NOT retrain or
  replace the production model -- consensus_rf_model.pkl and
  feature_names.json are unmodified (still v6, 11 features).

1. MOTIVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CRS-AI's 11 input features were originally selected by
  permutation importance computed on the same GroupShuffleSplit
  holdout later used to report the model's test performance
  (0.9474 ROC-AUC). The holdout is therefore not fully
  independent of the feature-selection decision -- a form of
  data leakage flagged by JBR Reviewer 2 (Major Point 3).

  This script re-runs feature selection under proper nested
  cross-validation: in each of 5 outer folds, permutation
  importance is computed on an inner validation split carved
  out of that fold's training data only, and the outer-test
  split never participates in the selection decision.

2. DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Candidate feature pool (18 features): the 11 production
  features plus the 7 candidates dropped in the v1->v2 cut
  (still computed by extract_features(), just not fed to
  the model): jtk_tau, jtk_period, harmonic_p_value, harmonic_r_squared, method_agreement, period_concordance, log_min_p_value.

  Outer loop:   5-fold StratifiedGroupKFold, grouped by gene
                (random_state=42), same grouping convention as
                train_consensus_model.py.
  Inner step:   80/20 GroupShuffleSplit (random_state=42) within
                each outer-train split. Permutation importance
                (n_repeats=10, scoring=ROC-AUC) computed on the
                inner validation split only.
  Selection rule (Supplementary Table S1 criterion): retain a
                candidate feature iff its mean permutation drop in
                ROC-AUC exceeds 1 SD of its own shuffle noise.
  Final fit:    Pipeline identical to train_consensus_model.py
                (SimpleImputer(constant=-999) -> CalibratedClassifierCV
                (RandomForest, isotonic, 5-fold internal StratifiedGroupKFold
                calibration)), trained on the FULL outer-train split using
                only the features selected by the inner step, then
                evaluated on the outer-test split.
  Aggregate:    Out-of-fold predictions from all 5 outer folds are
                pooled (each instance gets exactly one out-of-fold
                prediction) into a single ROC-AUC / F1 / Brier / Accuracy
                estimate with 1000-resample percentile bootstrap CI,
                directly comparable to the single-partition holdout
                reported in training_report.txt.
  External check: each outer fold's fitted model is also evaluated
                on GSE37332 (zebrafish), which never participated in
                training or feature engineering at any step.

  Training corpus: 5649 instances, 4691 gene/synthetic groups
  (identical generators and seeds as train_consensus_model.py).

7. INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The pooled nested-CV ROC-AUC is lower than the single-partition
  estimate reported in the paper, consistent with the reviewer's
  concern that the original holdout number was somewhat optimistic
  due to shared feature-selection/evaluation data.

  Feature-selection stability across outer folds indicates whether the
  11 production features represent a robust choice or an artifact of
  the single original train/test partition (Section 5 above).
