1. MOTIVATION AND DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  JBR review comment: CRS-AI's reported performance versus BioCycle/
  RhythmicDB reduces to how well CRS-AI recreates BioCycle's own
  classification, which the reviewer does not consider a true ground
  truth. This validation instead uses simulated waveforms whose
  rhythmicity is fixed at generation time (label = 1 iff a real 24h
  cosine component with amplitude = SNR x noise_std was injected;
  label = 0 iff amplitude = 0, i.e. SNR = 0). No inference procedure
  of any kind -- BioCycle, CRS-AI, or otherwise -- determines the
  label. This complements, and does not replace, the BioCycle-
  consistent validations already in training_report.txt and
  validate_external_holdout_gse37332.py.

  SNR levels (0 = arrhythmic ground truth): [0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0]
  Fixed observation-noise std: 1.5  (true amplitude = SNR x 1.5)
  True period jitter (rhythmic instances): 23-25 h
  Sampling: duration in [48.0, 72.0, 96.0] h, interval in [1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0] h (irregular per instance)
  Distortions (independently randomised per instance):
    Non-stationary linear trend: P=0.5, slope in (-0.08, 0.08) units/h
    Spike outliers (1-2 spikes, 6.0x local std): P=0.3
    Sampling gaps: P=0.4, drop fraction in (0.1, 0.35)
  Prediction path: core.meta_classifier.ConsensusClassifier.predict()
  (the same production entry point used by the app's AI Consensus
  panel) -- feature extraction and the trained Random Forest are
  reused unmodified; nothing about the model was reimplemented.

6. SUGGESTED REPORTING (JBR response letter)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    "To address the concern that CRS-AI's evaluation against BioCycle/
    RhythmicDB may be circular, we additionally validated CRS-AI on
    420 simulated ~24h waveforms spanning 7 signal-to-noise
    ratios (SNR=0, i.e. no true periodic component, through SNR=5),
    each independently corrupted with Gaussian noise, spike outliers,
    non-stationary linear trends, and sampling gaps. Ground truth was
    fixed at generation time and never inferred by any method. CRS-AI
    achieved AUROC=0.915 (95% CI 0.887-0.942)
    discriminating rhythmic from arrhythmic simulated series overall,
    with predicted probability increasing monotonically with true SNR
    and per-SNR-level AUROC approaching 1.0 at high signal strength
    (see Table/Figure), confirming that CRS-AI tracks genuine signal
    strength rather than recapitulating BioCycle-specific artifacts."
