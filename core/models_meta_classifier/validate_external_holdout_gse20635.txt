2. VALIDATION DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  GSE20635 adds a mammalian species and tissue combination not
  represented anywhere in CRS-AI's training or prior validation
  data:

    * Different species:  CRS-AI was trained on Mus musculus
      liver (GSE11516, GSE11923), Drosophila melanogaster clock
      neurons (GSE77451), and Homo sapiens blood (GSE39445), and
      externally validated on Danio rerio (GSE37332). Rattus
      norvegicus is phylogenetically closer to mouse and human
      than zebrafish, making this a complementary, higher-
      similarity mammalian generalisation test.

    * Different tissue:  Abdominal white adipose tissue is a
      peripheral metabolic clock tissue not present in the
      training corpus (liver, neurons, blood) or the zebrafish
      whole-organism validation.

    * True biological replication:  2-3 animals per timepoint,
      unlike the whole-organism zebrafish pools.

    * Different experimental platform:  GPL1355 Affymetrix Rat
      Genome 230 2.0 Array.

    * Zero data leakage:  GSE20635 was never accessed at any
      step of CRS-AI training, hyperparameter optimisation, or
      feature engineering. Labels were assigned entirely from an
      independent BioCycle analysis (RhythmicDB, E-GEOD-20635)
      after training was complete.

  Label quality: BioCycle-consistent non-rhythmic-from-absence
  labeling is used, matched at the probe level for the reasons
  given above -- the same underlying strategy used for CRS-AI
  v6 training and for the GSE37332/GSE3416 validations.
